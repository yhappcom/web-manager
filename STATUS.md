## 148 checkpoint
- Monitoring objectives are decomposed into observation freshness, ingestion/processing lag, coverage continuity, gap recovery, unobservability tolerance and per-decision evidence freshness; one universal timeout is rejected.
- Escalation is consequence- and evidence-role based. Low-risk diagnostic degradation does not block unrelated product capability; authority-critical uncertainty can gate only the affected release/recovery/remote-mutation capability.
- Coverage state from 147 is interpreted per decision: current-state recovery may restore a decision requiring only current state, but cannot restore one whose safety depends on transition history.
- Aggregate monitoring/error-budget health is operational evidence, not permission to accept a stale authority-critical decision.
- Warning, revalidation-required, capability-gated and incident/break-glass states are distinct. Alert acknowledgement or manual override does not itself restore assurance.
- No MintTap/LogMate production thresholds were invented. Actual limits depend on consequence, volatility, provider retention/replay, independent observation, threat context and product requirements.
- For PWA/EFB, server/provider monitoring, served release/SW publication, actual device SW/runtime activation and local operation/trust reconciliation remain separate clocks/evidence scopes.
