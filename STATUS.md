## Current integrated checkpoint — through 260
- Distribution success is not authority success; transport/cache freshness cannot elect canonical policy or prove semantic application.
- Consumer monotonic floors/checkpoints are security state; loss/reset/restore requires recovery rather than resetting the floor.
- Partial fleet convergence is explicit assurance debt; offline/unknown tails remain visible until governed disposition.
- Device retirement is an authorized lifecycle transition, not telemetry timeout; same physical hardware after erase/re-enrollment may be a new administrative incarnation.
- Data continuity can be preserved while authority continuity remains intentionally broken.
- Transport retry/idempotency, package/record identity, semantic duplicate detection and conflict resolution are distinct controls.
- Conflict resolution is a provenance-bearing decision; conditional writes prevent blind lost updates but do not elect semantic truth.
- Escalation does not inflate routing/support/admin roles into universal domain authority; reopening/supersession preserves historical decision provenance.
- Downstream revocation is a versioned convergence problem. Source correction, cache invalidation, delivery, receipt, semantic application, durable commit and acknowledgement are distinct states.
- Acknowledgement must be bound to recipient/incarnation, subject, projection/floor, consequence and current policy/verifier context. Client self-report is evidence, not authority.
- Historical ACK from an old incarnation remains provenance and is not automatically inherited by a successor incarnation.
- ACK proof validity, credential authorization, compromise interval, current applicability and recipient currentness are separate questions.
- Credential compromise scopes uncertainty; it neither validates every historical ACK nor proves every historical ACK false.
- Credential rotation establishes a successor authority epoch; it does not re-author historical ACKs.
- Compromised recipient/operator authority cannot be the sole authority that clears its own compromise or silently repairs its own disputed ledger mutations.
- Ledger correction preserves the original event and adds correction/supersession provenance.
- Compromise impact follows typed, consequence-bearing dependency edges rather than temporal/device/session correlation.
- Directly affected evidence, transitively affected closure/export claims, unknown dependencies and independent evidence remain separate states.
- Missing dependency provenance creates explicit uncertainty/assurance debt; it is neither proof of safety nor proof every candidate dependency existed.
- Reauthorization is new scoped evidence under a successor authority epoch; it does not relabel predecessor history.
- Closure is recomputed from current prerequisites. Positive successor evidence is insufficient where a controllable compromised predecessor path still works.
- Analytics, Web Push, Service Worker and session freshness can measure/accelerate convergence but cannot create semantic acknowledgement, dependency edges or compromise clearance.
