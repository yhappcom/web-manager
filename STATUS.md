## Current integrated checkpoint — through 270
- Composite `NORMAL` is a derived, revocable decision, not a new independent trust root; cache reuse retains prerequisite/epoch/topology/revocation dependencies.
- Revocation distribution is evidence transport, not self-authenticating truth. Material currentness needs authoritative source/scope, authenticity, freshness/currentness semantics and anti-rollback relation.
- Historical decision/status validity and current applicability are separate. Authentic old status remains evidence but cannot supersede newer authoritative state merely because it arrived later or was restored.
- Channel silence/partition is not proof that nothing was revoked. Where currentness cannot be established, affected high-consequence operations enter consequence-aware degraded/revalidation state; unique data and unaffected lower-risk capability remain distinct.
- Decision/revocation currentness cannot rely on TTL or wall-clock alone. Subject/scope, authority/policy/schema/configuration epochs, verifier context, topology and monotonic/anti-rollback generation/checkpoint may be material.
- Multiple delivery channels can share one registry/key/replica/deployment failure domain; transport diversity does not automatically provide evidence independence.
- Contradictory authenticated views are preserved and reconciled by authority/scope/lineage rather than arrival order or majority transport vote.
- Recovery/rejoin is forward reconciliation. A stale replica, PITR snapshot, manual import or long-offline client cannot roll already-established current revocation state backward.
- Push/broadcast invalidation is an optimization. Long-offline PWA safety requires authoritative stale/obsolete-generation rejection when a queued high-consequence operation eventually arrives.
- Queued payload/provenance can remain valuable after execution authority expires; `queue retained ≠ queue authorized`.
- Service Worker/controller/Cache Storage/network currentness is runtime evidence, not semantic authorization or revocation closure.
- If the revocation source itself is compromised, its own later `all clear` is insufficient; successor trust requires a path not solely controlled by the compromised source.
