## Current integrated checkpoint — through 239
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Corroboration is a claim-scoped dependency graph, not report count; majority is not authority.
- The graph itself is evidence, not truth or authorization. Authentic graphs can be incomplete, stale or semantically wrong.
- Independence is relative to a failure/adversary hypothesis and consequence cut and is bound to graph version/currentness.
- **There is no universal authoritative-source hierarchy.** Source authority is field-, scope-, generation-, time- and failure-hypothesis-specific.
- Reconciliation preserves **desired / declared / observed / effective** state rather than collapsing them prematurely.
- A nominal source of record inside the suspected failure domain cannot self-clear its own contradiction merely because it is normally authoritative.
- Conflicts are classified (temporal, generation/identity, scope, semantic, propagation, observation lag, unmodeled dependency, potential compromise/poisoning) before repair.
- Topology repair is append/supersede with provenance and decision lineage, not silent overwrite.
- Union, intersection and majority are unsafe generic reconciliation rules.
- Graph publication authority does not make graph facts true; runtime contradiction can reopen a claim without becoming policy authority.
- Repair is incomplete until affected consequence boundaries pass positive/negative effective-state validation.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad evidence retains graph/authority epoch; preserve unique local data before current bootstrap/re-admission.
