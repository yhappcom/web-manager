## Current integrated checkpoint — through 233
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Compromised normal authority cannot be the sole authority certifying its own replacement; recovery authority is independently protected, dormant and capability-bounded.
- Recovery material and authorization are separate; threshold is downstream of threat/failure-domain analysis.
- Recovery-root rotation is a versioned authority transition. Successor establishment, predecessor extinction and fleet convergence are separate gates.
- Recovery inventory is evidence, not authority; completeness requires multi-plane reconciliation and dormant does not mean extinct.
- **Destruction is not one boolean state.** USE-FENCED, REVOKED/DEAUTHORIZED, SANITIZED/CE, PROVIDER-DESTROYED, PHYSICALLY SANITIZED/DESTROYED and RESIDUAL-COPY-UNKNOWN are distinct claims.
- **Current-authority retirement and universal material erasure are separate assurance goals.** A predecessor can be safely fenced from current consequence while universal physical-copy erasure remains unprovable; neither claim substitutes for the other.
- **Scheduled destruction is transitional.** A reversible provider safety window is DESTRUCTION-PENDING, not destruction closure.
- Provider/HSM destruction evidence is scoped to the controlled object/path. Imported/exported/offline/backup copies remain separate discovery/destruction obligations.
- Destruction receipts and negative consequence tests are complementary: receipt does not prove stale-path rejection; rejection does not prove physical erasure.
- Backup/PITR/reimport paths must not resurrect accepted retired authority at rejoin.
- Residual-copy uncertainty is bounded assurance debt only when successor authority and all affected enforcement/restore paths demonstrably reject predecessor material; otherwise escalate to compromise/recovery.
- Preserve non-secret lineage/audit evidence without retaining retired private authority merely for auditability.
- PWA reinstall/cache clearing is not server recovery-key destruction evidence. Preserve unique long-offline local data, bootstrap current trust and re-admit queued consequences under current authority.
