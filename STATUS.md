## 133 checkpoint
- Current authoritative state, original intent, automated transformation, conflict adjudication and later correction are distinct provenance objects/activities.
- A correction is a new assertion; it must not rewrite history so a migrated/defaulted value appears to have been the user's original input.
- W3C PROV supplies a transferable conceptual distinction among entity/activity/agent/derivation/revision; no PROV serialization or RDF implementation is selected.
- Auditability does not imply full event sourcing or unlimited payload retention. Current projection can remain operational source of truth with bounded provenance for material transitions.
- Business provenance, security/application logging and analytics have different purposes, access and retention. Analytics must not become a shadow full-payload provenance store.
- Privacy minimization/storage-limitation and correction/accuracy concerns require purpose-bound provenance. No product jurisdiction or concrete retention period is inferred.
- Business deletion, sync tombstone, privacy erasure and provenance invalidation are separate operations; tombstone retention does not require retaining the full deleted payload.
- Local browser provenance inherits browser-storage durability/eviction uncertainty and is not an independent audit archive without runtime/storage evidence.
