## Current integrated checkpoint — through 281
- Composite `NORMAL` remains derived/revocable; historical/cached decisions do not become independent trust roots.
- Migration completion is consequence closure, not fleet simultaneity; offline/unknown clients may remain if stale authority cannot create material effects without current re-admission.
- Credential inventory is a lineage-and-acceptance graph; acceptance-boundary completeness is a maintained/versioned assurance claim.
- Topology evidence separates **authenticity, accuracy, authority and completeness**. A valid signature does not prove the statement accurate, authorized to normalize a path, or complete.
- Contradictory evidence remains first-class state; last-write-wins, newest-source-wins and majority-of-correlated-feeds are not generic adjudication rules.
- Assurance independence is threat/consequence-specific and modeled as a failure-domain vector, not a boolean or source count.
- An **independence budget** records which failure-domain separations a consequence class requires; it is not a universal numeric score.
- **Budget exhaustion is consequence-specific.** Losing one uniquely independent source can exhaust a high-consequence budget while losing several correlated diagnostic feeds may not.
- Explicit conceptual operating states now include `NORMAL`, `DEGRADED`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONTAINED`, `RECOVERY` and evidence-based re-entry. These are not prescribed product enums.
- **Graceful assurance degradation is capability decomposition, not `NORMAL` with a warning.** Local read/capture/preservation may remain defensible while remote publication, destructive mutation or stale recovery authority is fenced, subject to product-specific validation.
- The mapping from consequence → assurance requirement → degraded capability is itself consequence-bearing, versioned authority; emergency mode cannot silently lower assurance forever.
- Recovery drills should use a ladder: model review → tabletop → simulation/shadow evaluation → isolated functional exercise → bounded runtime drill where justified → restore/long-offline rejoin exercise. A tabletop PASS is not runtime PASS.
- Drill mechanisms such as test credentials, bypass headers or debug verifier modes are temporary authority and require explicit retirement/negative proof.
- Recovery closure requires successor positive proof **and** predecessor/suspect-authority negative proof; a working V2 alone does not retire V1.
- Restore/PITR is an assurance-plane mutation event and must not resurrect stale verifier policy, cached `NORMAL`, exhausted independence assumptions or drill controls.
- A long-offline PWA client is neither a clean recovery root nor disposable. Preserve unique records/provenance, seek current admission, reconcile queued operations and reject obsolete authority.
- Re-entry is evidence-based and generation-bound, not time-based. Residual UNKNOWNs remain explicit rather than being normalized by schedule pressure.
