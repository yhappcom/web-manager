## Current integrated checkpoint — through 278
- Composite `NORMAL` remains derived/revocable; historical/cached decisions do not become independent trust roots.
- Migration completion is consequence closure, not fleet simultaneity; offline/unknown clients may remain if stale authority cannot create material effects without current re-admission.
- A migration bridge is a bounded capability, not a new root; scope, generations, consequence classes, translation rules, non-transitivity and retirement must be explicit.
- Browser logout, IdP logout, access-token revocation, refresh/grant revocation, RP/server-session invalidation and background/recovery credential retirement are distinct surfaces.
- Credential inventory is a lineage-and-acceptance graph, not a token table. Provider exports, code search, runtime telemetry, secret/service inventory, admin/recovery paths, job/deployment topology and active negative probes are complementary evidence channels.
- Acceptance-boundary completeness is a maintained/versioned assurance claim over consequence-bearing effectors, not a static endpoint list or one-time clean diagram.
- Material deployments, removals, restores and rollbacks must update/re-attest topology, be explicitly classified non-material, or remain UNKNOWN with consequence-proportionate containment.
- Hidden bypasses can be semantic even when endpoints are inventoried: skipped middleware, legacy headers, over-broad service credentials, background consumers and privileged internal calls remain first-class topology concerns.
- `not observed` is weaker than `cannot create a material effect`; zero stale/bypass traffic does not prove dormant authority is absent.
- Orphaned authority is an unresolved risk state, but orphaned credentials must not be conflated with user-data validity or used to justify destructive offline-data cleanup.
- Dormant privileged credentials and rare recovery/compatibility paths are prioritized by consequence, not frequency.
- Predecessor/UNKNOWN negative proof at representative material boundaries anchors bounded closure; test equivalence requires shared enforcement/deployment/consequence evidence.
- Long-offline PWA clients are latent callers/unknown-tail state, not hidden server boundaries. On reconnect they require current admission and operation-level reconciliation while unique local records/provenance are preserved.
- Service Worker/runtime topology and server authority topology are separate assurance domains; neither proves the other current.
- Backup/PITR/rollback are topology/currentness mutation events and may preserve historical artifacts but must not resurrect obsolete authority or retired acceptance paths.
- Telemetry coverage is itself an assurance claim; monitoring effectiveness and monitoring completeness remain distinct.
- High-consequence recovery/migration may degrade while unique offline data and safe preserve/read/recovery capability remain available.
