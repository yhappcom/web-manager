## Current integrated checkpoint — through 242
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Recovery authority is exceptional bootstrap authority, not parallel everyday governance. Availability of recovery credentials does not itself authorize invocation.
- Ceremony authorization, factual evidence quality, successor publication, consequence acceptance and recovery closure are separate claims.
- Ceremony participants, credentials, offline media/instructions and recovery policy all have currentness; physical possession or authentic old material does not establish current authority.
- Recovery independence is evaluated against the suspected failure hypothesis. Offline/different-account placement alone does not prove independence from IAM/PAM/provider/personnel/device/custody cuts.
- Ceremony transcripts bind incident/recovery ID, predecessor generation, recovery-policy generation, participant roles, evidence references, exact successor proposal digest, publication and validation/closure evidence without retaining live private authority.
- Two plausible successors are a fork (`SUCCESSOR-CONFLICT`), not a majority/timestamp/reachability contest. One valid signed successor view does not prove non-equivocation.
- Canonical successor publication is incomplete until relevant consequence boundaries enforce the monotonic successor floor and reject predecessor/competing branches.
- Emergency recovery authority is explicitly retired after normal governance returns; service restoration alone is not closure.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad state preserves unique data/evidence and requires canonical successor/bootstrap evidence before consequence-bearing replay; it never elects a branch by stale local preference.
