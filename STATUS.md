## 171–204 integrated checkpoint
- Authority, evidence, observation and convergence remain separate layers. Offline-first utility preserves unique local data without making stale authority current.
- Continuity generation issuance, admission, distribution and recovery effectiveness are distinct; conflicting valid-looking heads are incident evidence, not majority elections.
- Currentness floors survive ordinary control-plane loss in an independent-enough failure domain but remain rollback evidence rather than unilateral authority.
- Reconstitution/break-glass authority is exceptional, incident-bound, scoped and extinguishable; recovery completion requires negative authorization evidence across applicable API/session/region/PITR paths.
- Authority retirement is a convergence claim across explicit consequence-bearing enforcement surfaces. Revocation requested/accepted/observed/enforced are distinct; residual authority is actively revocable, verified bounded-expiry or UNKNOWN.
- Partial revocation/currentness-plane outage is handled per operation and enforcement domain. A failure in the mechanism that could supply fresher authorization cannot increase the lifetime, scope, privilege or generation of the last trustworthy evidence.
- Time-bounded authorization separates issuer statement, verifier wall time, elapsed duration and uncertainty. Reboot/restore/process-epoch changes invalidate elapsed-time assumptions unless continuity is independently established.
- Time-source authenticity, correctness, independence and freshness are distinct. Secure transport does not make a compromised/correlated source truthful.
- Temporal/currentness epoch issuance, admission, distribution, observation and enforcement are distinct. Cross-region floor split-brain is an authorization incident, not a clock/majority election.
- A compromised source/trust path cannot unilaterally authorize its successor. Routing/retry must not search for a stale permissive region.
- **Witness/floor evidence is an anti-rollback constraint and contradiction oracle, not unilateral successor authority.**
- **Witness independence is multidimensional: administration, keys, storage/PITR, control plane, network, time and recovery dependencies matter. Separate endpoint/vendor names do not prove independence.**
- **Authentic stale distribution remains stale after restore/partition. Signature validity does not reset the recipient's admitted floor.**
- **Higher generation does not automatically win. E+1/E+2 ordering requires authenticated authorized lineage/consistency; numeric maximum, wall-clock, arrival order, region majority and telemetry count are not authority elections.**
- **Partition healing is staged and monotonic: quarantine/re-admission → current floor/lineage → enforcement proof → traffic reopening. Network reconnection alone must not create a transient retired-authority acceptance window.**
- PWA local preservation, local use, deferred intent and remotely effective mutation remain separate. Long-offline clients heal forward under current authority without rewriting historical uncertainty.
