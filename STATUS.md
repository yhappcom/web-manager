# Stage 10 — App-Company Web Strategy & Growth
**ACTIVE — FOUNDATION/PRACTITIONER.**

077 — **App-Company Web Portfolio & Lifecycle Strategy — PASS.**

Integrated portfolio model:
`company identity → product identity → task/proof resources → distribution/store handoff → support → governance/trust → operational status/release → lifecycle/retirement`.

Retained judgments through 077:
- company identity, product identity, campaign, support, status, release and governance are distinct semantic responsibilities even when they share a visual system/domain;
- one-app architecture should not bind the entire company domain identity to the first product if future portfolio growth is plausible;
- resource/URL identity is a lifecycle asset and should survive ordinary navigation redesign;
- website and stores share product truth but are separate distribution/presentation systems;
- store CTA activation ≠ store view ≠ download ≠ install ≠ launch ≠ retained use;
- proof assets require claim scope, product/release relevance, ownership and freshness triggers;
- public HTTPS resource, verified app association and native route correctness are separate layers;
- Apple Universal Links and Android App Links support verified web↔native associations, but browser-valid fallback and route validation remain required;
- PWA is a product/task capability set, not a company-wide website label;
- prelaunch/launch/postlaunch is a cross-surface consistency lifecycle, not merely a publishing date;
- support, status, changelog/release, account/data and governance surfaces solve different user tasks;
- localization readiness requires product/store/support/governance/proof/measurement readiness, not translation alone;
- Marketing owns channels/positioning; Web Manager owns truthful receiving resources, task routing, continuity and web evidence;
- retirement requires support/search/URL/data/deep-link continuity planning; deleting or homepage-redirecting all retired URLs is not automatically correct.

New reusable controls:
- **Resource Identity Record** — purpose, scope, canonical URL intent, lifecycle, locales, native association, store destinations, dependencies, measurement, replacement/redirect policy and owner.
- **Locale Readiness Contract** — product/store/core page/support/governance/proof/search/deep-link/measurement readiness.
- **Portfolio Consistency Matrix** — company web/product web/store/native-or-PWA/support-governance truth checked across identity, availability, claims, platform, privacy, links, locale and lifecycle.
