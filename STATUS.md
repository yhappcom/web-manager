## Current integrated checkpoint — through 276
- Composite `NORMAL` remains derived/revocable; historical/cached decisions do not become independent trust roots.
- Planned rollover, predecessor compromise, overlap compromise, recovery-root compromise, organizational custody loss, custody/provider/org migration overlap and migration-bridge retirement are distinct trust problems.
- Recovery authority is a custody/dependency graph; key bytes, named custodians or provider reachability alone do not establish recoverability or current authority.
- Custody generation/currentness is an organizational admission claim, not a timestamp, generation integer or provider-local assertion.
- Planned old/new overlap may be necessary, but overlap does not create permanent co-equal authority or permit either side to self-elect.
- Provider/control-plane migration and organizational-authority migration are separate state dimensions; tenant transfer does not automatically transfer emergency mandate.
- A migration bridge is a bounded capability, not a new root: scope, generations, consequence classes, translation rules, non-transitivity and retirement conditions must be explicit.
- Browser logout, IdP logout, access-token revocation, refresh/grant revocation, RP/server-session invalidation and background/recovery credential retirement are distinct surfaces.
- Cross-provider invalidation is a credential/acceptance graph problem; one provider's successful revocation cannot prove another provider or application-local session is neutralized.
- Long-offline PWA clients cannot be assumed to receive revocation signals; authoritative online boundaries must reject obsolete authority or require current re-admission before material effects.
- Migration completion is consequence closure, not fleet simultaneity: an offline/unknown tail can remain if stale authority cannot create material effects without current re-admission.
- Zero observed stale traffic is weak negative evidence; active negative proof and topology/acceptance-boundary coverage are required for material closure.
- Bridge retirement requires successor positive proof plus bridge/predecessor negative proof and rollback resistance.
- Backup/PITR and long-offline PWA state may preserve historical evidence and unique data but must not roll current custody/session/token/bridge authority backward.
- High-consequence recovery/migration may degrade while unique offline data and safe preserve/read/recovery capability remain available.
- Service Worker/MDM/push/API/provider tooling can retain/distribute migration material but cannot elect custodians, establish organizational authority or prove credential invalidation.
