## 171–196 integrated checkpoint
- Authority, evidence, observation and convergence remain separate layers. Offline-first utility preserves unique local data without making stale authority current.
- Continuity generation issuance, admission, distribution and recovery effectiveness are distinct; conflicting valid-looking heads are incident evidence, not majority elections.
- Continuity-content lineage, admission-policy lineage and admission-key/role lineage remain distinct. Policy/key succession is a higher-order authenticated transition.
- Compromised admission material cannot solely bless its successor. If no disputed branch can be uniquely established, explicit reconstitution preserves the unresolved interval as UNKNOWN.
- The currentness floor must survive ordinary application/control-plane loss in an independent-enough failure domain, but remains rollback evidence rather than unilateral authority.
- Reconstitution authority is an exceptional security plane: scoped, generational, activatable for a bounded incident and retireable after successor normal authority is established; it is not a permanent super-root.
- Break-glass material/administrator privilege/outage evidence does not itself authorize activation. Activation is a distinct incident-bound security transition with bounded scope and termination conditions.
- Recovery completion requires privilege extinction evidence: retired exceptional authority must fail consequence-bearing replay across API/session/region/PITR paths. Hidden UI, incident closure, credential rotation or absence of observed use is insufficient.
- **Recovery authorization evidence, emergency identity and drill evidence are distinct assurance objects. Ordinary audit loss does not justify unaudited recovery; independently retained evidence must survive relevant failures without becoming a new issuance authority.**
- **Emergency-identity independence is failure-domain-specific. A second account/provider label does not prove independence if federation, MFA/recovery, vault/KMS, network, personnel or administrative recovery remains correlated.**
- **Drill assurance is layered: tabletop, credential/retrieval readiness, bounded technical activation, post-use extinction/anti-resurrection and compound-failure evidence answer different questions. A successful login drill is not end-to-end recovery assurance.**
- Long-offline PWA clients preserve local records, obtain current authenticated successor policy/recovery state, rebootstrap forward and re-admit consequence-bearing work. Cached client state never activates recovery authority and drill metadata never becomes current authority.
