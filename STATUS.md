- **Custody succession is governed security-policy succession, not merely handing a key/file/account to another employee.** Personnel/provider loss never authorizes silent threshold or trust-policy weakening.
- **Provider migration is not recovery-authority succession.** Current Apple platform support can migrate qualifying managed devices between management services, but eligibility, provider behavior and product topology remain runtime evidence.

- A survivable bootstrap design needs a pre-governed path/basis outside any ordinary provisioning failure being modeled; fallback availability does not redefine trust.
- Long-offline/company-iPad recovery preserves unique data first, then bootstraps current authority, re-enrolls/reprovisions where required and re-admits consequence-bearing work without rewriting uncertain history.
