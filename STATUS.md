## 145 checkpoint
- Assurance propagation uses a small typed graph: CLAIM, EVIDENCE, DEPENDENCY, EXCEPTION, POLICY/GENERATION and ENVIRONMENT/SCOPE nodes with explicit SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, DEPENDS_ON, OBSERVED_UNDER, NARROWS/WAIVES, REQUIRES and scoped SUPERSEDES relations.
- A dependency change invalidates/narrows directly dependent evidence first; claim state is then recomputed. Invalidation is not equivalent to failure and historical evidence is retained.
- Transitive invalidation follows typed semantic prerequisite edges, not raw graph reachability. This prevents both stale green assurance and blanket retesting.
- Current claims with no applicable evidence cannot remain green. Orphan evidence with unknown scope/provenance cannot be attached by label similarity.
- Cycles are not automatically defects, but circular assurance where A proves B and B proves A without an independent evidence/root basis is not acceptable.
- Contradictory evidence is preserved. A newer PASS does not erase a prior FAIL without scoped resolution evidence/rationale.
- Graph completeness is not assumed. Zero unexpected impact after a material dependency change can indicate a missing edge; unexpectedly huge fan-out can indicate over-broad semantics.
- For PWA/EFB, server, WebKit/Service Worker/MDM and local-storage evidence remain separately scoped. Targeted invalidation preserves irreplaceable local data while remote authority can remain gated.

## Persistent guards added by 145

`dependency changed ≠ unaffected evidence stale`.  
`evidence invalidated ≠ historical evidence deleted`.  
`supporting evidence invalidated ≠ claim proven false`.  
`one supporting edge remains ≠ claim fully assured`.  
`graph reachable ≠ semantically dependent`.  
`new evidence exists ≠ old contradiction disappears`.  
`dependency restored ≠ old evidence automatically revalidated`.  
`orphan evidence exists ≠ claim supported`.  
`cycle exists ≠ defect`.  
`cycle is sole proof basis ≠ assurance`.  
`latest evidence wins ≠ contradiction resolved`.
