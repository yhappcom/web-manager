## Current integrated checkpoint — through 241
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Topology repair remains a governed, versioned transition over evidence; approval authorizes publication but does not prove topology truth.
- Governance identity is layered: **principal → account/federation → credential/session/token → role → delegation → governance generation/root**. These lifecycles must not be collapsed.
- Credential compromise fences consequence-bearing credentials/sessions and triggers review of approvals in the suspect interval; password reset alone is not assumed sufficient.
- Role succession closes only after successor establishment plus negative retirement of predecessor reach, including latent PAM/provider recovery, delegated grants and long-lived sessions.
- Delegation is explicit, scoped, currentness/time bound and non-transitive unless expressly authorized; composed effective privilege matters more than grant count or role labels.
- Pending approvals bind to governance generation. Material role/threshold/delegation/recovery changes require re-evaluation rather than silent carry-forward.
- Planned governance-root rotation and suspected root/quorum compromise are distinct. A suspected compromised threshold cannot establish trustworthy continuity merely by certifying its own successor; recovery needs an independent pre-established basis.
- Loss/unavailability of quorum does not silently redefine the normal threshold. Emergency/recovery authority remains explicit and creates assurance debt until normal governance is restored.
- Governance currentness is monotonic; valid old signatures do not make an old governance generation current.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad state preserves unique data and stale evidence, authenticates successor governance/bootstrap, then re-admits queued operations under current policy; it cannot vote global governance backward.
