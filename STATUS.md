## 168 integrated checkpoint
- A notification is a hint that authoritative state may have changed, not authoritative state, identity proof, adjudication result or mutation credential. Sensitive status resolves in an authenticated first-party context.
- Separate notification destination, destination validation/currentness, delivery event, channel-safe content, status authority and mutation/adjudication authority. `delivered` does not prove the intended human read the message.
- NIST SP 800-63B-4 is the current final identity baseline: consequential authenticator/recovery events require independent account notifications; CSPs support multiple notification addresses and provide repudiation instructions. This supports fraud detection, not treating an email/phone/push endpoint as universal authority.
- External email/SMS/push content should minimize lock-screen/inbox disclosure. A case/deep-link reference grants no entitlement by itself; current session/capability authorization remains required.
- Contact-address changes are security-sensitive lifecycle events. Validate new destinations, preserve surviving repudiation/recovery paths and do not trust a newly supplied destination automatically during an account-takeover complaint.
- Support cannot ask for passwords/recovery secrets/authenticator-binding secrets or turn an unsolicited MFA approval into `verification`. Suspicious users need an independently discoverable first-party route rather than reliance on caller/message-provided contact information.
- Apple/WebKit confirms standards-based Web Push for iOS/iPadOS Home Screen web apps from 16.4 with direct-user-interaction permission and lock-screen/Notification Center presentation. Push can be delayed/offline-delivered subject to TTL, so an authentic push may be stale.
- PWA push is optional attention delivery, never the sole status/redress/recovery path. Notification click must resolve current authority outside cached SW/IndexedDB state; unique unsynced local flight data remains independent from notification/session failure.
- Delivery/repudiation analytics remain purpose-limited operational evidence and must not become identity/adjudication truth or a marketing case-history graph.
