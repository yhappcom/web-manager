## 167 integrated checkpoint
- Redress access is consequence-scoped authorization, not a universal maximum-proofing event. Separate subject resolution, claimant authentication/proofing, capability authorization, abuse/fraud confidence and adjudication authority.
- Select proof proportionate to harm for the requested capability. Public discovery, low-disclosure intake, authenticated explanation, contest submission, sensitive identity/delegation changes and consequence-bearing disposition need not share one assurance requirement.
- Before entitlement, prevent account/decision enumeration through prose/status/timing/redirect discrepancies. After entitlement, preserve 166's meaningful explanation; anti-enumeration is not a reason to make legitimate post-auth explanations useless.
- Layer rate limiting, replay/idempotency and bot/risk controls without treating them as identity truth or creating attacker-induced permanent lockout. Preserve an accessible alternate recovery/redress path.
- NIST SP 800-63 Rev.4 is the current final baseline: identity controls should be risk-based, usable/privacy-enhancing; 63A-4 requires easy-to-find/use redress and privacy/data minimization; 63B-4 separates phishing resistance from manual OTP and addresses rate limiting/authenticator compromise.
- WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.8 applies to authentication: do not make memory/transcription/puzzle barriers the sole path; preserve password-manager/autofill/paste support and accessible recovery alternatives.
- Support, representative delegation, account recovery and policy adjudication remain distinct authorities. A persuasive support interaction must not silently create delegation or disposition authority.
- A compromised-account complaint cannot require the compromised authenticator as the only route, but a newly supplied email/phone is not automatically trusted. Recovery establishes a new authority/session generation rather than reviving stale sessions.
- Long-offline PWA session/device possession is historical evidence, not current remote redress authority. Preserve unique unsynced local data and locally drafted contests while revalidating current authority outside cached Service Worker state.
- Abuse/fraud analytics may measure aggregate workflow health but must not become canonical identity evidence or a permanent subject-level fraud/adjudication dossier.
