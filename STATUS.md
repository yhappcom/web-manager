## 171–181 integrated checkpoint
- Federation evidence, local delegation, authentication/session state, emergency authority, recovery authority and offline observation are separate authority layers.
- Security-policy publication, regional admission, actual enforcement, client observation and convergence evidence are distinct distributed states.
- Offline-first utility does not require stale-authority fail-open. Unique local records/drafts may remain usable while consequence-bearing remote operations wait for current server admission.
- Availability/reliability objectives do not authorize weaker security. Temporary exceptions/break-glass are bounded executable generations with hard expiry/revocation, rollback resistance and evidence.
- Emergency-authority compromise recovery is trust reconstitution, not credential rotation. A compromised emergency/recovery root cannot solely bless its successor.
- Recovery-root survivability is continuity of governed authority/evidence, not duplicate credentials. People, IdP, provider, administrator, site/device, legal entity, evidence store and policy/currentness can be correlated failure domains.
- Organizational/personnel succession is a new eligibility/custody event, not inheritance of predecessor credentials, sessions or emergency generation.
- Recovery ceremony is a versioned state machine with inherited-privilege freeze, surviving-basis verification, successor generation, invalidation, convergence, enforcement proof and closure.
- **Recovery liveness is not authority weakening.** Participant/resource availability, eligibility, quorum rule, independence, escalation and runtime admission are separate states.
- **Timeout is an escalation/reassessment signal, not an authorization primitive.** Elapsed time, outage duration, backlog or business urgency cannot by themselves lower quorum or waive independence.
- **Deadlock-safe recovery uses pre-established alternate paths.** If the organization requires survival of quorum loss, policy-reconstitution authority must itself be a separately governed continuity capability; it cannot be invented after the quorum is lost.
- Safe non-completion is preferable to unilateral takeover when no legitimate path exists. Local/read/export work can remain available while privileged remote consequences are paused.
- Reconnect after recovery/succession requires current server authority before privileged queue drain; stale SW/IndexedDB/client clocks cannot create or import quorum authority.
