## 171–182 integrated checkpoint
- Federation evidence, local delegation, authentication/session state, emergency authority, recovery authority, constitutional recovery policy and offline observation are separate authority layers.
- Security-policy publication, regional admission, actual enforcement, client observation and convergence evidence are distinct distributed states.
- Offline-first utility does not require stale-authority fail-open. Unique local records/drafts may remain usable while consequence-bearing remote operations wait for current server admission.
- Availability/reliability objectives do not authorize weaker security. Temporary exceptions/break-glass are bounded executable generations with hard expiry/revocation, rollback resistance and evidence.
- Emergency-authority compromise recovery is trust reconstitution, not credential rotation. A compromised emergency/recovery root cannot solely bless its successor.
- Recovery-root survivability is continuity of governed authority/evidence, not duplicate credentials. People, IdP, provider, administrator, site/device, legal entity, evidence store and policy/currentness can be correlated failure domains.
- Organizational/personnel succession is a new eligibility/custody event, not inheritance of predecessor credentials, sessions or emergency generation.
- Recovery ceremony is a versioned state machine with inherited-privilege freeze, surviving-basis verification, successor generation, invalidation, convergence, enforcement proof and closure.
- Recovery liveness is not authority weakening. Timeout, outage duration, backlog and urgency cannot create authority or lower quorum.
- Deadlock-safe recovery uses pre-established alternate paths; policy-reconstitution authority cannot be invented after the ordinary quorum is lost.
- **Recovery-policy change is constitutional authority, not ordinary configuration.** Policy semantics, generation, governance authority, governance key, publication, regional admission and runtime enforcement are distinct.
- **The current administrator, failed quorum or compromised governance root cannot unilaterally weaken its successor rules.** Approval must bind the exact successor artifact/generation and be evaluated under the correct governing policy.
- **Governance-key rotation is not policy authorization.** Planned key/algorithm migration and compromise reconstitution are distinct; a compromised old key signing a new key is not sufficient successor evidence.
- Constitutional generations move forward at the authority layer. Historical signed policy, cache or PITR cannot silently restore an easier executable policy.
- Safe non-completion is preferable to unilateral takeover when no legitimate constitutional path exists. Local/read/export work can remain available while privileged remote consequences are paused.
- Long-offline PWA clients may skip policy/key generations; reconnect obtains current server authority before privileged queue drain. Stale SW/IndexedDB/client clocks are observations, not constitutional oracles.
