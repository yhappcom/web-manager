## Current integrated checkpoint — through 228
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Assurance-debt scope and partial reauthorization are dependency-graph claims; UNKNOWN/CONTRADICTED material edges prevent false narrow-scope recovery.
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Mixed-generation proof publication is invalid; proof issuance and consumption are separate TOCTOU boundaries.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; historical proof can remain valid for audit while becoming inadmissible for current consequence.
- Availability leadership is distinct from security-floor authority; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- **Ordinary floor storage cannot prove its own anti-rollback currentness after rollback/PITR.** Recovery needs authenticated minimum-currentness evidence outside the relevant rollback/failure domain or a governed successor-recovery transition.
- **Anti-rollback anchors are minimum-currentness witnesses, not full policy engines.** Anchor authenticity does not prove every policy/dependency/enforcement path is current.
- **Anchor independence is failure-domain diversity, not replica count.** Shared IAM, keys, admin, deployment or recovery can collapse apparently separate anchors into one failure domain.
- **Anchor disagreement is a reconciliation/fork condition, never permission to select the lower floor for availability.**
- Anchor loss must not become a synchronous availability choke point for safe capabilities: authenticated cached minima can preserve bounded operation, but missing refresh never lowers the floor.
- **Floor-history compaction is a security transition.** It may summarize history only when successor/retirement lineage and unresolved contradiction/debt remain verifiable.
- **Partition/recovery rejoin is a proof bundle, not a health check.** Current floor, writer fence, dependency generations, reconciliation and negative stale-writer rejection are separate requirements.
- Positive evidence that the new writer works does not prove the old writer is extinct; stale-writer rejection at actual side-effect boundaries is required where feasible.
- Service Worker/IndexedDB/cache state may contribute client-local historical evidence but is not sovereign server authority.
- Long-offline return preserves unique local data first; queued consequence-bearing operations are re-admitted under current authority.
- Total normal-anchor loss requires governed recovery/successor authority rather than “newest local timestamp” fallback.
