Integrated portfolio/release model:
`company identity → product identity → resource/proof truth → store/distribution state → support/governance → release/rollout population → locale/region → measurement → maintenance/retirement → URL/data continuity`.

Retained judgments through 078:
- release is not one atomic event; build/review/release/rollout/region/web/support/proof/deep-link/PWA states can diverge;
- approved/uploaded ≠ universally available; mixed-version populations are normal during phased/staged rollout;
- product capability, platform, country/region, store state, locale and support readiness are separate dimensions;
- default/custom store listings and campaign pages are presentation variants bounded by canonical product truth;
- proof assets require claim scope, release lineage, locale, freshness trigger and owner;
- portfolio growth requires explicit product-truth ownership rather than duplicated page-level truth;
- localization needs semantic/release lineage, not only translation completion;
- distribution removal ≠ installed-user/support/data lifecycle completion;
- URL retirement/migration must preserve user intent; irrelevant mass homepage redirects are not healthy lifecycle management;
- PWA origin deployment ≠ all installed clients updated; worker/cache/schema/client versions create another mixed-version surface;
- acquisition/experiment gains do not override accessibility, performance, security, privacy, support or reliability guardrails.
