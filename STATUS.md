## 157 checkpoint
- Recovery-ceremony abort is an authoritative monotonic security transition, not a UI cancellation.
- Once ceremony generation N is authoritatively aborted, its approvals and derived temporary authority must not regain execution authority through retry, stale replicas, restore, rollback, Service Worker/cache state or offline replay.
- Restart is a new ceremony generation with fresh target/package/currentness/participant verification; predecessor approvals are historical evidence only and cannot contribute to the new quorum.
- Partial effects are classified as uncommitted, reversible, compensatable, irreversible or unknown; distributed recovery is not assumed to have atomic rollback.
- Abort requires four separate obligations: stop new consequential effects, neutralize/quarantine derived authority, reconcile already-created effects, and preserve non-secret forensic evidence.
- Clean restart requires predecessor containment plus a surviving independently governed authority path; permanent fail-closed deadlock is an architecture defect, not justification for reactivating compromised authority.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad data remains preservable independently of recovery authority; stale local ceremony state cannot override server-side abort.

## Persistent guards added by 157
`abort recorded ≠ partial authority neutralized`.
