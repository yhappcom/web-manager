# Stage 9 closure
075 — **Analytics, Measurement & Experimentation Evidence Foundations — PASS.**

076 — **Measurement Reliability, Causal Inference & Pre-Launch Integration — PASS / STAGE 9 GATE CLOSED.**

Integrated evidence chain:
`decision → target population/task → product truth → observable signal → collection → missingness/selection → transformation/modeling → metric/uncertainty → alternatives → causal design where needed → guardrails → decision → intervention → re-observation`.

Retained judgments through 076:
- reality, observable population, collected data, processed report, modeled estimate and causal effect are separate layers;
- metric reliability requires semantic/version stability, denominator, coverage, late/duplicate policy, missingness, transformation/reporting knowledge and reconciliation;
- report completeness does not imply population completeness;
- consent, blockers, browser/platform, offline operation, identity boundaries and instrumentation failures can create systematically missing populations;
- observed vs modeled, sampled/thresholded and vendor data-quality states must be preserved where applicable;
- occurrence time and ingestion time can diverge, especially for offline/PWA telemetry;
- attribution remains a bounded credit/observation model, not a reconstructed private causal journey;
- causal claims require designs that reduce alternative explanations; randomization unit, eligibility, exposure, contamination and guardrails must be explicit;
- blocking/stratification can address pre-specified nuisance variation but post-hoc segment hunting is not equivalent;
- statistical detectability and practical value are distinct; non-significance does not prove equality;
- not every question warrants A/B testing, especially with low niche traffic, known correctness/accessibility/security obligations or infeasible randomization;
- pre-launch measurement planning now starts from decisions/page-family tasks, not from vendor event catalogs;
- Analytics Evidence Registry now includes denominator, missingness, occurrence/ingestion policy, duplicate policy, modeled/sampled state, identity scope, reconciliation and decision threshold.
