## 155 checkpoint
- Technical escrow survival and legitimate organizational recovery authority are separate claims.
- Recovery semantics distinguish custody, approval, execution, audit and succession; high-consequence recovery must not accidentally collapse them into one unchecked actor.
- Succession is versioned/currentness-aware: predecessor custody is explicitly retired, and stale offline shares/credentials remain suspect until rotation/resealing establishes the new custody generation.
- Quorum, split knowledge and dual control reduce selected single-person risks but do not prove incident/target/package/participant legitimacy or independent failure domains.
- Emergency recovery authority is purpose-bounded and temporary; it cannot silently rewrite its own succession basis or become an ordinary production fallback plane.
- Organizational-loss exercises deliberately remove normal experts/admins and must prove both recoverability and non-capture.
- Recovery closure includes post-recovery rotation/resealing and a negative test that predecessor/emergency authority no longer authorizes.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad state may preserve irreplaceable data but cannot nominate custodians or satisfy organizational succession.
