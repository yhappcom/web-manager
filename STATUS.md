## Current integrated checkpoint — through 231
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Compromised normal authority cannot be the sole authority certifying its own replacement; recovery authority is independently protected, dormant and capability-bounded.
- Recovery material and authorization are separate; threshold is downstream of threat/failure-domain analysis.
- **Recovery-root rotation is a versioned authority transition, not key replacement alone.** Successor establishment, predecessor extinction and fleet convergence are separate gates.
- **Old/new recovery-root overlap must be bounded and directional.** Migration need does not justify indefinite dual standing authority.
- **Custodian succession changes the failure-domain claim.** The same numerical threshold after personnel/provider/organizational change does not prove equivalent independence.
- Personnel departure is not closure until latent identity/admin/HSM/vault/material access is retired with evidence.
- Planned rotation and compromise recovery are distinct. A suspected predecessor must not use the convenient ordinary rotation path as sole authority for its successor.
- **Permanent threshold loss does not authorize threshold reduction.** A separately legitimate loss-recovery basis is required; exact product mechanism remains OPEN.
- Long-offline clients may skip several recovery-root generations. Preserve unique local data first, then verify authenticated successor lineage/checkpoint or enter governed BOOTSTRAP-REQUIRED before remote consequence.
- Rotation-history compaction must retain enough bridge and retirement evidence; absence of a bridge is not permission to choose the newest discovered root.
- Organizational/provider custody transfer is an authority migration requiring failure-domain and authorization revalidation even when key bytes are unchanged.
- Recovery authority needs periodic custody/policy/crypto/bridge review, but review cadence is not a reason for empty ceremonial rotation.
