- Historical ACK from an old incarnation remains provenance and is not automatically inherited by a successor incarnation.
- Export remediation distinguishes controlled replacement/notification/acknowledgement from external copies outside technical control. Closure is obligation-scoped and must not claim remote deletion that cannot be proven.
- Analytics, Web Push and Service Worker delivery can measure/accelerate convergence but cannot create semantic acknowledgement.
