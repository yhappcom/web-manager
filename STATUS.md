- Durable topology admission requires accountable ownership, purpose, consequence/capability scope, lifecycle/review triggers, retirement path and approval provenance proportionate to risk.
- Shadow dependencies are not automatically malicious; preserve unique data/provenance while fencing unauthorized mutation/publication where needed.
- Emergency/manual paths create bounded exception debt, not permanent architectural precedent. Historical success does not backdate authorization.
- Exception expiry ends exceptional authority, not data existence. Normalization requires successor-current plus consequence-scoped predecessor fencing/removal/residual evidence.

- Exception-debt budgets are consequence-aware governance envelopes, not universal ticket quotas or semantic authorization scores.
- Renewal is a successor authorization decision; missed review, runtime necessity or beneficiary request cannot create implicit extension.

- Recurrence, age, concentration, critical-workflow dependence, missed normalization, contradiction and material assumption change can force architecture review; a review trigger is not destructive-shutdown authority.
- Legitimate prolonged contingency requires current bounded authority, compensating controls, periodic review and credible exit/recovery evidence; stale temporary labels do not prove temporary architecture.
- Budget exhaustion or exception expiry does not authorize destruction of unique offline data/provenance.
