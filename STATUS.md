## Current integrated checkpoint — through 240
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Corroboration is a claim-scoped dependency graph, not report count; the graph itself remains evidence rather than truth/authorization.
- Source authority is field/scope/generation/time/failure-hypothesis specific; desired/declared/observed/effective state remain distinct.
- Topology repair is append/supersede with provenance and effective-state validation, not silent overwrite.
- **Repair publication is a governed versioned state transition, not an administrator edit.**
- Proposal, evidence attachment, approval, publication and validation are distinct capabilities; separation is consequence/threat-model dependent.
- Dual authorization is useful only when approvers are meaningfully distinct for the relevant failure hypothesis; account count/signature count alone does not prove independence.
- Governance approval authorizes publication but does not make topology assertions factually true.
- Every repair proposal binds to an explicit base graph generation and governance-policy generation; stale concurrent proposals require rebase/reconciliation rather than blind overwrite.
- Graph currentness is monotonic. Intentional content rollback is a new successor generation, not permission to reinstall an old generation as current.
- Emergency repair authority is bounded/visible and creates explicit assurance debt when normal independence cannot be met; availability pressure does not silently lower normal thresholds.
- Repair closure requires positive/negative effective-state validation independent enough to challenge the failure hypothesis under investigation.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad evidence retains graph/authority epoch; preserve unique local data, authenticate successor lineage/bootstrap, then re-admit queued operations under current policy.
