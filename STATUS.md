## Current integrated checkpoint — through 230
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Ordinary floor storage cannot prove its own anti-rollback currentness after rollback/PITR; recovery needs authenticated minimum-currentness evidence outside the relevant failure domain or governed successor recovery.
- Authentic anchor disagreement is not an availability choice; freshness, telemetry majority and timestamp recency cannot erase an authentic contradiction.
- A suspected/compromised normal anchor set cannot be the sole authority certifying its own replacement. Total normal-anchor compromise/loss requires an independently governed bootstrap/recovery basis.
- Compromise recovery establishes successor current authority without rewriting fork history; obsolete branches require negative fencing at consequence-bearing boundaries.
- **Recovery material and recovery authorization are separate.** A key/share/HSM capability does not itself authorize a recovery event.
- **Recovery custody independence is a failure-domain claim.** Personnel count or physical separation alone does not establish independence when identity, administration, vault/HSM, provider or recovery dependencies remain shared.
- **Recovery authority should be dormant and capability-bounded.** It should establish/retire trust authority, not become a standing alternate product-mutation path.
- **Threshold is downstream of threat/failure-domain analysis.** `k-of-n configured` does not prove `k` independent compromise domains, and threshold satisfaction does not replace policy authorization.
- **Successor discovery is not successor authentication.** DNS/API/push/MDM/support/telemetry may announce recovery but cannot establish authority when they share the compromised trust cut.
- Recovery ceremony evidence should bind compromise scope, governing policy generation, participants/threshold, successor, minimum floor/checkpoint, effective generation and closure obligations; exact production schema remains OPEN.
- Long-offline return preserves unique local data first; cached historical root/floor/Service Worker state does not grandfather remote consequence. Bootstrap current successor trust, migrate required state, then re-admit queued consequence individually.
- Recovery testing must validate custody/threshold/ceremony/bootstrap without normalizing production emergency authority into a routine bypass.
- Successor establishment, obsolete-authority extinction and fleet convergence are separate gates; unresolved tails remain assurance debt.
- Recovery authority itself requires rotation/compromise/loss governance rather than being treated as an immortal final root.
