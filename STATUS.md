## Current integrated checkpoint — through 232
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Compromised normal authority cannot be the sole authority certifying its own replacement; recovery authority is independently protected, dormant and capability-bounded.
- Recovery material and authorization are separate; threshold is downstream of threat/failure-domain analysis.
- Recovery-root rotation is a versioned authority transition. Successor establishment, predecessor extinction and fleet convergence are separate gates.
- Custodian succession changes the failure-domain claim; permanent threshold loss does not authorize threshold reduction.
- **Recovery inventory is evidence, not authority.** Inventory metadata/reference systems must not become new stores of private recovery material merely for convenience.
- **Completeness requires multi-plane reconciliation.** Declared policy/IaC, HSM/KMS/vault, IAM/PAM/provider recovery, backup/PITR/media, custody/personnel, audit/use and negative retirement evidence have distinct blind spots.
- **Dormant does not mean extinct.** Zero observed use is weak evidence for rarely exercised recovery capability.
- Explicit inventory states distinguish CURRENT, RETIRED-VERIFIABLE, RETIREMENT-PENDING, ORPHAN, UNKNOWN and LOST; binary active/inactive is insufficient.
- Orphan discovery triggers containment/provenance analysis, not automatic authority promotion or destructive deletion.
- Backup/PITR/offline media belong in authority analysis whenever restoration can resurrect private recovery capability or administrative reach.
- Inventory attestations bind scope, generation, source coverage, provenance, observation interval, blind spots and contradictions; a signed completeness boolean is insufficient.
- Inventory uncertainty never lowers thresholds or appoints a successor. Degraded operation remains capability-scoped and preserves unique local data.
- Long-offline PWA stale-tail state is tracked separately from recovery-key inventory; browser-local historical trust cannot appoint current recovery authority.
- Rotation/succession inventory closure requires expected-current reconciliation plus negative retired-path/resurrection evidence.
