## 150 checkpoint
- Policy rollout is separated into structural/schema validation, replay/fixture differential testing, non-authoritative live shadow evaluation, and bounded authoritative rollout.
- Shadow evaluation never grants/denies the request, mutates trust/recovery state, clears gates, or becomes fallback authority when the authoritative evaluator fails.
- Decision diff compares outcome plus determining policy/prerequisite/error basis. Permit expansion, new restriction, same-result basis change, evaluator/UNKNOWN change, recovery-path regression and stale-client incompatibility are distinct classes.
- Diff risk is consequence-based rather than count-based. One remote-reset permit expansion can outweigh thousands of benign denials.
- Zero observed diff proves only no observed difference within the sampled evidence envelope; rare recovery/offline states require fixtures or isolated validation.
- Bounded rollout cohort assignment is authority-bearing where it changes authorization; clients cannot self-select a weaker policy generation or rollout cohort.
- Rollback is a security transition. A predecessor that is operationally known-good but cryptographically/security-wise retired cannot be resurrected merely to recover availability; forward-fix/containment/manual recovery may be required.
- PWA policy generation, server evaluator generation, served app/SW generation and physical iPad runtime generation remain separate state. Online canary health does not prove long-offline PWA compatibility.

## Persistent guards added by 150
`policy validates ≠ policy behavior safe`.  
`shadow evaluated ≠ shadow authorized`.  
`same final decision ≠ same decision basis`.  
`small diff count ≠ low-risk diff`.  
`zero observed diff ≠ semantic equivalence`.  
`representative traffic ≠ exhaustive state space`.  
`canary healthy ≠ offline fleet compatible`.  
`candidate deployed ≠ candidate authoritative everywhere`.  
`rollback config restored ≠ predecessor authority safely restored`.  
`client reports candidate generation ≠ server should trust candidate decision`.  
`shadow unavailable ≠ use candidate as fallback`.  
`known-good operationally ≠ still-authorized cryptographically/security-wise`.
