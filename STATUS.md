## 171–206 integrated checkpoint
- Authority, evidence, observation and convergence remain separate layers. Offline-first utility preserves unique local data without making stale authority current.
- Witness membership and threshold are versioned authority policy; witness loss does not authorize threshold reduction and retired quorum evidence cannot regain current remote authority.
- Ordinary witness succession and exceptional reconstitution are distinct. **Threshold/total witness compromise or unrecoverable quorum loss is a trust-anchor change, not an ordinary update.**
- **A failed predecessor witness plane cannot become the sole authority for its own replacement. Reconstitution requires a separately governed recovery anchor/channel appropriate to the modeled failure.**
- **HTTPS, mirror majority, highest generation, client wall clock, telemetry popularity and support-page discovery are not generic trust-reset authorities.**
- Recovery-package distribution and authorization are separate; authorized-but-unreachable does not permit downgrade to reachable stale material.
- Recovery packages bind explicit reconstitution identity, scope, successor policy and durable recovery generation/floor. A random package nonce alone is not replay protection for zero-state clients.
- Current, restored/rollback and new/zero-state clients have different bootstrap evidence. Zero-state clients require an authentic initial anchor/provisioning path and cannot infer legitimacy from newest-looking network state.
- Rebootstrap is one-way for current authority: successor-positive proof must be paired with retired-policy negative proof across scoped enforcement domains, restore/PITR and offline-client reconciliation.
- Long-offline PWA clients preserve unique data first, isolate stale trust/session state, bootstrap current authority, reject retired witness/recovery policy and re-admit consequence-bearing queues without rewriting missed history.
