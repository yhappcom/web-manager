## Current integrated checkpoint — through 279
- Composite `NORMAL` remains derived/revocable; historical/cached decisions do not become independent trust roots.
- Migration completion is consequence closure, not fleet simultaneity; offline/unknown clients may remain if stale authority cannot create material effects without current re-admission.
- Credential inventory is a lineage-and-acceptance graph, not a token table; acceptance-boundary completeness is a maintained/versioned assurance claim, not a static endpoint list.
- Material deployments, removals, restores and rollbacks must update/re-attest topology, be explicitly classified non-material, or remain UNKNOWN with consequence-proportionate containment.
- Hidden bypasses can be semantic even when endpoints are inventoried: skipped middleware, legacy headers, over-broad service credentials, background consumers and privileged internal calls remain first-class topology concerns.
- Topology evidence now separates **authenticity, accuracy, authority and completeness**. A valid signature primarily authenticates a statement/source; it does not prove the statement accurate, authorized to normalize a path, or complete.
- Material change lineage should connect authorized intent, source/config revision, produced/deployed artifact, target/generation, observed effect, consequence classification and verifier/policy generation where applicable. These may be separate artifacts but must remain reviewably linked.
- The producer of a topology change must not be the sole assurance basis that certifies the change legitimate under the same compromise model. Multiple feeds sharing one control plane are correlated evidence, not independent proof.
- Discovery sources are evidence channels, not authority-election channels. `discovered` or `already deployed` does not mean approved/current; unexplained material paths remain UNKNOWN until reconciled.
- Poisoned discovery includes omission as well as false addition. A healthy/signed scanner can still suppress a hidden worker or misclassify a material path.
- Contradictory topology evidence is preserved as a first-class state; last-write-wins, newest-source-wins and majority-of-correlated-feeds are not generic adjudication rules.
- Attestation/verifier policy is itself consequence-bearing authority. An attester may verify evidence without gaining unilateral power to redefine trusted sources, consequence classes, generations or exceptions.
- Cryptographic provenance cannot rescue a compromised semantic root. A compromised attester/control plane cannot self-clear by issuing a fresh signed `NORMAL` statement.
- Transparency/append-only consistency can strengthen anti-equivocation evidence but does not confer semantic legitimacy: `logged ≠ authorized`, `globally consistent ≠ correct`.
- `not observed` remains weaker than `cannot create a material effect`; telemetry coverage is itself an assurance claim.
- Long-offline PWA clients are latent callers/unknown-tail state, not hidden server boundaries. Rejoin consumes current topology/admission and operation-level reconciliation while unique local records/provenance are preserved.
- Service Worker/client-runtime provenance and server authority topology are separate assurance domains; a current HTTPS-delivered worker cannot normalize an unexplained server acceptance path.
- Backup/PITR/rollback may restore old attestations, signer mappings and verifier policy but must not restore their current authority. Restore invalidates currentness until reconciled against current trust basis.
