## 112 material findings
- recovery availability and recovery abuse resistance are separate properties. Human/provider-assisted recovery can resolve lost-control cases but is also a social-engineering trust bridge;
- recovery notification is detection/repudiation evidence, not authorization. Post-action notice cannot substitute for evidence before irreversible domain/trust/account changes;
- exceptional authority should be non-self-attesting where feasible: the actor performing recovery should not be the only source asserting that its activation/actions were legitimate;
- recovery actions should be staged by reversibility and blast radius: observe/preserve → contain → regain narrow control → prepare replacement authority → activate high-impact change → reconcile → retire emergency authority;
- delay/hold can create detection time but is not intrinsically secure; it must be evaluated with independent observation and containment of ongoing compromise;
- registrar/provider policy is part of the threat model. MFA/locks reduce risk but do not prove support-assisted takeover impossible or guarantee restoration;
- recovery-factor independence is about failure/authority domains, not factor count. Two channels recovered through one compromised IdP/mailbox are not independent;
- repudiation has hard limits after exfiltration, hostile-code delivery, trust-root activation or destructive key loss, so irreversible steps need stronger pre-action evidence;
- PWA compromise can persist in Service Worker/cache/IndexedDB/session/trust state after provider/domain recovery; preserve authoritative local records while quarantining uncertain authority;
- exact recovery evidence should bind event, claimant/role evidence class, provider/path, pre/post authority generations, action scope, independent observation and affected artifacts/sessions/clients without logging secrets.

## Persistent guards added by 112
`recovery path available ≠ recovery path abuse-resistant`.  
`human-assisted recovery ≠ high-assurance recovery`.  
`support agent accepted claimant ≠ organization independently authorized destructive change`.  
`notification delivered ≠ action authorized`.  
`notification after destructive change ≠ preventive control`.  
`audit record exists ≠ audit record independent of the actor being audited`.  
`authorized to inspect ≠ authorized to replace trust root`.  
`waiting period exists ≠ attacker cannot wait`.  
`registrar MFA enabled ≠ support-assisted takeover impossible`.  
`two factors ≠ two independent failure domains`.  
`event repudiated ≠ compromise effects automatically reversed`.  
`case number exists ≠ exact destructive action and resulting authority are evidenced`.  
`fraudulent recovery reversed at provider ≠ installed hostile state eradicated`.  
`wipe removes hostile cache ≠ wipe is acceptable if it destroys authoritative local records`.
