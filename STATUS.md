## 171–175 integrated checkpoint
- Federation is bounded issuer-specific evidence, not evergreen local delegation. External assertion, local authority mapping, delegation generation, browser/session/token state and offline observation remain distinct.
- Federation metadata, JWKS, trust chains and local trust-anchor configuration are versioned security state. HTTPS reachability or valid signatures alone do not establish current semantic trust.
- Trust-policy publication is a distributed security-state transition: authoring, authorization, publication, regional admission, enforcement, client observation and convergence evidence are distinct.
- Availability is consequence-specific. Transport, application, data, identity, trust-currentness, authorization and mutation availability are separate states.
- Offline-first utility does not require stale-authority fail-open. Unique local records/drafts may remain usable while consequence-bearing remote operations wait for current server admission.
- Content-staleness tolerance and authority-staleness tolerance are separate budgets. Emergency revocation can require stricter currentness than ordinary outage handling.
- RTO, RPO, authority-currentness, emergency-revocation propagation, reconciliation and local-data-durability objectives are separate. Numeric objectives require actual BIA/product/provider evidence and remain OPEN.
- Dependency failure propagates through both semantic dependencies and shared resources. IdP/resolver/status latency can exhaust API pools; retry and queue recovery can become secondary outages.
- Isolation exists to preserve safe capability classes, not to create cached-authority fail-open. Retry ownership, timeout budgets, resource isolation, load shedding and progressive queue drain require implementation evidence.
- Health is layered: reachability, dependency readiness, security-floor currentness, actual enforcement/admission and reconciliation/backlog state. Generic green health cannot prove authorization safety.
- Degraded-mode observability should prefer low-cardinality capability/dependency/generation aggregates. Telemetry absence, lag or sampling cannot be silently interpreted as healthy/current.
- Client/PWA telemetry supports diagnosis but is never the authority oracle for server enforcement or convergence.
- Service Worker/app-shell availability and semantic authorization-policy availability/convergence remain separate.
- No generic claim is made that iPadOS refreshes federation metadata, keys, tokens, policy or Service Worker in the background. Physical Safari/Home Screen/MDM transfer remains OPEN.
