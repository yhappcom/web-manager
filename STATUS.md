## Current integrated checkpoint — through 277
- Composite `NORMAL` remains derived/revocable; historical/cached decisions do not become independent trust roots.
- Migration completion is consequence closure, not fleet simultaneity; offline/unknown clients may remain if stale authority cannot create material effects without current re-admission.
- A migration bridge is a bounded capability, not a new root; scope, generations, consequence classes, translation rules, non-transitivity and retirement must be explicit.
- Browser logout, IdP logout, access-token revocation, refresh/grant revocation, RP/server-session invalidation and background/recovery credential retirement are distinct surfaces.
- Credential inventory is a lineage-and-acceptance graph, not a token table. Provider exports, code search, runtime telemetry, secret/service inventory, admin/recovery paths, job/deployment topology and active negative probes are complementary evidence channels.
- `not observed` is weaker than `cannot create a material effect`; zero stale traffic does not prove dormant authority is absent.
- Orphaned authority is an unresolved risk state, but orphaned credentials must not be conflated with user-data validity or used to justify destructive offline-data cleanup.
- Dormant privileged credentials and rare recovery/compatibility paths are prioritized by consequence, not frequency.
- Acceptance-boundary enumeration plus predecessor/UNKNOWN negative proof anchors bounded closure; disconnected credential bytes need not be remotely erased if authoritative boundaries reject them.
- Long-offline PWA clients cannot be assumed to receive revocation signals; on reconnect they require current admission and operation-level reconciliation while unique local records/provenance are preserved.
- Backup/PITR and historical verification material may preserve old artifacts but must not resurrect obsolete credential/session/bridge authority as current.
- Telemetry coverage is itself an assurance claim; `100% of instrumented endpoints` does not prove topology coverage is complete.
- High-consequence recovery/migration may degrade while unique offline data and safe preserve/read/recovery capability remain available.
