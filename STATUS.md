## 171–180 integrated checkpoint
- Federation evidence, local delegation, authentication/session state, emergency authority, recovery authority and offline observation are separate authority layers.
- Security-policy publication, regional admission, actual enforcement, client observation and convergence evidence are distinct distributed states.
- Offline-first utility does not require stale-authority fail-open. Unique local records/drafts may remain usable while consequence-bearing remote operations wait for current server admission.
- Availability/reliability objectives do not authorize weaker security. Local preservation, remote read, mutation admission, authority currentness, reconciliation, recovery proof and accessibility remain distinct objective families.
- Temporary exceptions and break-glass capabilities are bounded executable generations with server-side admission, hard expiry/revocation, rollback resistance, evidence and post-use closure.
- Compensating-control configuration is not assurance; material failure/UNKNOWN cannot silently preserve an exceptional path that depends on that control.
- Approval independence is evaluated across authority/failure domains rather than click/account/share count.
- Emergency-authority compromise recovery is trust reconstitution, not credential rotation. A compromised emergency/recovery root cannot be the sole authority that blesses its successor.
- **Recovery-root survivability is continuity of governed authority/evidence, not duplicate credentials.** People, IdP, provider, administrator, site/device, legal entity, evidence store and policy/currentness can be correlated failure domains.
- **Organizational/personnel succession is a new eligibility/custody event, not inheritance of predecessor credentials, sessions or emergency generation.** Domain, billing, MDM, device or provider-account ownership does not by itself prove successor authority.
- Recovery ceremony is a versioned state machine: declare/scope → freeze inherited privilege → establish surviving recovery basis → verify current ceremony policy → authorize successor generation → publish/admit → invalidate/reconcile → prove enforcement → progressive reopen → close/replenish/test.
- Recovery evidence must establish what survived outside the failed domain while minimizing personal/secret data. Missing/suspected evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- Reconnect after succession requires current server authority before privileged queue drain; Service Worker/application/data migration and trust reconstitution remain separate.
- No generic claim is made that iPadOS background execution, push, MDM, device ownership, cached SW/IndexedDB state or a physical company iPad can maintain/prove emergency or successor authority.
