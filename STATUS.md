## Current integrated checkpoint — through 234
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Compromised normal authority cannot be the sole authority certifying its own replacement; recovery authority is independently protected, dormant and capability-bounded.
- Recovery-root rotation is a versioned authority transition; successor establishment, predecessor extinction and fleet convergence are separate gates.
- Recovery inventory is evidence, not authority; completeness requires multi-plane reconciliation and dormant does not mean extinct.
- Destruction is not one boolean state. USE-FENCED, REVOKED/DEAUTHORIZED, SANITIZED/CE, PROVIDER-DESTROYED, PHYSICALLY SANITIZED/DESTROYED and RESIDUAL-COPY-UNKNOWN are distinct claims.
- Current-authority retirement and universal material erasure are separate assurance goals.
- **Destruction evidence is provenance-bound.** Bind receipt/attestation to exact logical authority, object/version/material ID, provider/account/region or HSM domain, lifecycle phase and observation time.
- **Provider lifecycle evidence and application authority-retirement evidence are different planes.** Provider receipt does not prove stale verifier rejection; negative rejection does not prove provider physical erasure.
- **Independence is claim-relative.** Provider-native evidence can be the strongest practical evidence for provider-internal lifecycle state, while successor/floor enforcement and negative consequence tests should independently establish application retirement.
- **Delayed provider erasure and current-authority fencing run on separate clocks.** Authority retirement may close while material destruction remains pending; neither claim is upgraded into the other.
- **Backup/PITR/reimport resurrection closure is a rejoin property.** Recovered environments remain quarantined until current floor/successor state is acquired and predecessor negative tests pass.
- Imported/recreated identical key bytes do not automatically restore current authority; generation/floor/policy lineage governs admission.
- Preserve a minimal non-secret closure package beyond ordinary audit-log retention without retaining retired private capability.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad stale tails are useful authority-rejection tests, not server-key destruction evidence; preserve unique local data and re-bootstrap current trust.
