## 171–172 integrated checkpoint
- Federation is bounded issuer-specific evidence, not evergreen local delegation. External assertion, external relationship evidence, local authority mapping, local delegation generation, browser/session/token state and offline observation remain distinct.
- Trust issuers per claim class/purpose. Authentication trust does not imply employment, representative or case-delegation authority.
- Federation metadata, JWKS, trust chains and local trust-anchor configuration are versioned security state. HTTPS reachability or a valid signature alone does not establish current semantic trust.
- RFC 8414 issuer binding is a security invariant: metadata whose returned issuer does not exactly match the expected issuer must not be used. Protected-resource and authorization-server bindings remain distinct.
- OpenID Federation 1.0 is Final (2026-02-17); OpenID Federation for OpenID Connect 1.1 is Final (2026-05-05). Valid federation membership/trust-chain evidence still does not prove local LogMate delegation.
- Normal key rotation, provider migration and compromise recovery are separate protocols. A compromised old anchor/provider cannot be the sole authority that blesses its successor.
- Concurrent materially different valid-looking trust views are reconciliation/incident conditions, not last-write-wins. Divergence alone does not prove attack; rollout/cache/migration can also produce transient split views.
- Long-offline PWA clients may preserve unique local records/drafts, but cached federation metadata/JWKS/delegation state is historical observation. Consequence-bearing queued work must be re-evaluated against the current server-side trust/authority floor before replay.
- No generic claim is made that iPadOS refreshes federation metadata, keys, tokens or Service Worker in the background. Physical Safari/Home Screen/MDM transfer remains OPEN.
