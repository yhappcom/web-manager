## Current integrated checkpoint — through 237
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.
- Recovery authority is independently protected, dormant and capability-bounded; recovery-root rotation is a versioned authority transition.
- Recovery inventory is evidence, not authority; dormant does not mean extinct.
- Destruction is not one boolean state; current-authority retirement and universal material erasure are separate assurance goals.
- Closure proof is a historical claim plus current dependency judgment, not an authority credential; contradiction is appended, not history-rewritten.
- Contradiction intake is an untrusted evidence-ingress plane, not authority; plausible high-consequence evidence can justify least-destructive reversible containment before attribution completes.
- False-reopen floods are a control-plane DoS risk; semantic deduplication and bounded processing must not silently discard unique high-consequence evidence.
- **Corroboration is a claim-scoped dependency graph, not a report count.** Independence is relative to the failure/adversary hypothesis and affected consequence cut.
- **Majority is not authority.** One direct, authentic, current consequence-boundary observation can outweigh many weak correlated `normal` observations for the exact claim it tests.
- **Many identities/hashes/regions/dashboards do not imply independent evidence.** Shared IAM, provider-admin, MDM, parser, deployment, clock, collector or upstream event can collapse apparent diversity.
- Strong conflicting evidence enters scope/currentness/fork investigation; it is not resolved by arithmetic voting.
- Independence itself drifts and must be versioned/revalidated after material dependency change.
- Long-offline PWA/iPad stale tails remain candidate contradiction evidence; preserve unique local data before bootstrap/re-admission.
