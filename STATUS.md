## 171–205 integrated checkpoint
- Authority, evidence, observation and convergence remain separate layers. Offline-first utility preserves unique local data without making stale authority current.
- Continuity/currentness issuance, admission, distribution, observation and enforcement are distinct. Conflicting valid-looking heads are incident evidence, not majority elections.
- Witness/floor evidence is anti-rollback constraint and contradiction evidence, not unilateral successor authority. Witness independence is multidimensional across administration, keys, storage/PITR, control plane, network, time and recovery.
- Authentic stale distribution remains stale after restore/partition; higher generation does not automatically win; partition healing is quarantine/re-admission first and consequence-bearing traffic reopening last.
- **Witness membership and threshold are versioned authority policy, not ambient runtime configuration.**
- **Witness-policy succession requires authorization under the currently admitted predecessor policy plus admissibility under the successor policy; a new witness set cannot self-appoint merely by satisfying its own signatures.**
- **Quorum unavailability does not authorize threshold reduction. Witness loss, witness compromise and threshold compromise are different recovery states.**
- **Retired witness/quorum evidence may remain historically valid but cannot regain current remote authority after a successor policy is admitted.**
- **Two threshold-valid conflicting views are equivocation/incident evidence until authorized lineage resolves them; wall-clock, arrival order, region majority, telemetry popularity, witness-set size and numeric generation are not generic elections.**
- When ordinary quorum continuity cannot be trusted, exceptional reconstitution must be explicit and incident-bound; it must not fabricate predecessor quorum evidence.
- Long-offline PWA clients preserve unique data, update witness-policy/currentness lineage, reject retired quorum authority and re-admit consequence-bearing queues under current authority without rewriting missed history.

## Freshness / current external precedent
- TUF Specification **1.0.36, modified 2026-08-05**, defines root metadata as versioned key/threshold policy and requires each successor root N+1 to be signed by threshold under both the currently trusted root N and the new root N+1, with exact incremental version succession. This is strong transfer precedent for governed witness-policy succession, not a MintTap architecture prescription.
- TUF distinguishes ordinary key loss/rotation from threshold compromise; threshold compromise may require out-of-band recovery and is not safely repaired by silently lowering the threshold.
