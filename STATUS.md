- Recovery is trust reconstitution, not credential rotation; compromised roots cannot solely bless successors; quorum outage/timeout does not create authority.
- Constitutional policy change is higher-order authority. Approval binds to exact immutable artifact identity/lineage; build, signature, publication, admission and enforcement remain separate evidence layers.
- Transparency is detection evidence, not authority. Witness independence is a failure-domain property; monitor silence is not proof of no conflict; conflicting signed checkpoints survive rotation/recovery.

- Archive integrity is not archive authority; historical verification is a bounded read-only capability; deletion/hold/sanitization are governed lifecycle states.
- Archive migration is provenance, not trust renewal. Re-attestation is a later bounded claim and cannot recreate original trust or erase INVALID/UNKNOWN.
- Timestamp/preservation services are bounded evidence providers, not original business authority or current admission authority. Compromise creates an evidence-backed uncertainty interval that successor issuance cannot retroactively repair.
- Provider exit/portability transfers evidence plus applicable historical validation context; it does not transfer predecessor current signing authority.
- Portability is a demonstrated recovery capability, not an export feature. Package completeness, independent custody, independent interpretability and executed recovery are separate proof dimensions.
- Continuity/escrow independence is failure-domain-specific. Different copies/providers/regions do not prove independence when IdP, KMS, administrator, deletion authority, DNS/email, billing or recovery paths remain correlated.
- **Continuity custody is a survivability/evidence plane, not a current-authority plane. Independence from the incumbent does not prove the continuity copy is correct or fresh.**
- **Package integrity, authenticity, completeness, set consistency, freshness and current authority are separate properties. Immutability protects against later mutation; it does not prove that poisoned or stale content was not locked in.**

- PITR/restores reconcile against surviving current preservation/security/deletion/hold/continuity floors; an internally consistent historical registry is not current authority.
- PWA clients may retain old provider roots/tokens/checkpoints/package observations but are not trusted clocks, preservation services, escrow authorities or authority votes.
