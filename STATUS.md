- Canonical successor authorization and fleet/runtime convergence are separate claims. Popularity cannot substitute for valid recovery lineage; valid lineage cannot prove all boundaries converged.
- Convergence is consequence-scoped. Material boundaries must accept the current successor and negatively reject predecessor/competing branches; a stale read-only copy is not automatically a live authority path.
