- Assurance-debt scope and partial reauthorization are dependency-graph claims; UNKNOWN/CONTRADICTED material edges prevent false narrow-scope recovery.

- Admission uses scoped authenticated monotonic floors; failover does not resurrect retired authority.

- Partial recovery is permitted only where dependency-graph evidence proves the capability trust cut lies outside the compromised authority domain; UNKNOWN material edges block narrow reauthorization.
- Successor publication is insufficient until obsolete fork branches/writers are negatively fenced at consequence-bearing side-effect boundaries where feasible.
- Transparency and telemetry are evidence planes, not implicit fork-resolution authorities.
- Long-offline return preserves unique local data first; cached historical branch/floor/Service Worker state does not grandfather remote consequence.
- Recovery remains assurance debt until stale branches, credentials, writers, regions and offline tails are reconciled or explicitly bounded.
