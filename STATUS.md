## 171–194 integrated checkpoint
- Authority, evidence, observation and convergence remain separate layers. Offline-first utility preserves unique local data without making stale authority current.
- Continuity generation issuance, admission, distribution and recovery effectiveness are distinct; conflicting valid-looking heads are incident evidence, not majority elections.
- Continuity-content lineage, admission-policy lineage and admission-key/role lineage remain distinct. Policy/key succession is a higher-order authenticated transition.
- Compromised admission material cannot solely bless its successor. If no disputed branch can be uniquely established, explicit reconstitution preserves the unresolved interval as UNKNOWN.
- The currentness floor must survive ordinary application/control-plane loss in an independent-enough failure domain, but remains rollback evidence rather than unilateral authority.
- **Reconstitution authority is a fourth, exceptional security plane: scoped, generational, activatable for a bounded incident and retireable after successor normal authority is established. It must not become a permanent super-root.**
- **An independent floor can itself be stale, rolled back, poisoned before immutability, spliced, incomplete or correlated with another floor. Authenticity/immutability do not prove currentness or admissibility.**
- **Mutually inconsistent authenticated floors are reconciled by lineage/policy/failure-domain evidence, never storage count, maximum generation or newest local timestamp. If no unique lineage survives, preserve UNKNOWN and reconstitute forward.**
- **Catastrophic recovery converges through an admitted successor policy/currentness floor plus proof that restored regions enforce it. Service health alone is not policy convergence. Availability pressure does not authorize floor lowering.**
- Long-offline PWA clients preserve local records, obtain current authenticated recovery/policy state, rebootstrap forward and re-admit consequence-bearing work. Cached client state never becomes the recovery floor.
