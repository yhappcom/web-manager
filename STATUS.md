## 126 checkpoint
- System/data restoration is narrower than organizational-authority recovery; replacement infrastructure cannot self-attest itself into historical authority.
- Recovery authority must derive from surviving provenance sufficiently independent of the failed identity/control domain, or the affected claim remains explicitly uncertain.
- Alternate copies/providers are not independent when the same SSO, recovery mailbox, administrators, device or authority plane controls them.
- Provider account recovery/identity re-proofing can restore bounded account control but does not reconstruct every historical authorization, signing, deployment or data-provenance claim.
- Bootstrap authority is capability-scoped: historical verification, backup decryption, domain/origin restoration, production publication, remote write enablement and destructive mutation need not share one recovery privilege.
- Emergency/break-glass authority remains temporary; successful bootstrap must normalize into a new explicit authority graph and retire emergency credentials.
- Domain/origin recovery does not recover installed PWAs; stale offline clients still require current fail-closed re-entry and compromise-era outbox reconciliation.
- EFB-like local read/export/recovery can remain separable from remote mutation authority during organizational recovery where product/legal policy permits.
- Assurance depth is consequence-based; organizational survivability does not imply enterprise PKI/HSM/escrow by default.
- Track C now owns destructive organizational-loss drills that remove original people, identity tenants, providers and recovery channels to prove non-circular recovery.
