## Current integrated checkpoint — through 227
Prior guards and conclusions remain canonical in their numbered research artifacts. Current high-value integration:
- Assurance-debt scope and partial reauthorization are dependency-graph claims; UNKNOWN/CONTRADICTED material edges prevent false narrow-scope recovery.
- Dependency discovery is multi-plane and no discovery source proves its own completeness.
- Proof validity is claim-, dependency-, generation- and evidence-specific; stored PASS is not automatically current.
- High-consequence proof carries predecessor/supersession lineage, assessment fences, dependency-generation vector, observation interval, event provenance and oracle set.
- Mixed-generation proof publication is invalid; individually authentic observations do not form a coherent proof when material dependencies changed without bounded revalidation.
- Proof issuance and consumption are separate TOCTOU boundaries.
- **Admission now has a scoped authenticated monotonic floor.** Once a consequence-bearing scope learns a higher authority/policy/proof floor, stale regions/caches/clients cannot lower it merely because older evidence remains authentic.
- **Monotonicity applies to authority lineage/current selection, not to wall-clock time or permanently shrinking product capability.** A newer authorized policy may deliberately permit a capability; it does not resurrect an older proof.
- **Proof consumer fencing is distinct from proof authenticity.** A valid proof and credential do not authorize a stale writer whose admission/writer epoch is obsolete.
- **Availability leadership is distinct from security-floor authority.** A failover survivor must reconcile current floor evidence or remain within a pre-authorized degraded capability ceiling.
- **No global synchronous consensus per operation is assumed.** Capability/trust-cut scoping can preserve local/read/data-capture availability while consequence-bearing remote operations require sufficient currentness.
- Floor publication, distribution, effective consumer enforcement and negative stale-proof rejection are separate convergence claims.
- Rollback/PITR may restore old data/application state but must not silently lower the security/admission floor.
- Service Worker generation is client-relative evidence, not global server authorization authority.
- Long-offline return preserves unique local data first; queued consequence-bearing operations are re-admitted under current authority rather than creation-time proof.
- Writer fencing and operation identity/idempotency solve different failure classes and both may be required.
